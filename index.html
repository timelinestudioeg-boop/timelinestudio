01 About Me

Creative Thinking. Precise Execution.

I'm Mahmoud Elyan, a freelance Video Editor and Motion Designer based in Egypt, with 1–2 years of experience working across different video styles and creative projects.

My approach starts with understanding the client's vision, audience, and objectives. From there, I shape the footage, pacing, transitions, motion elements, and visual details into a cohesive final piece.

I work across real estate videos, commercial advertisements, social media content, speed ramps, transitions, and motion graphics. I also explore AI-assisted tools to animate still images, develop visual transitions, and create additional possibilities for real estate and creative projects.

I believe effective video editing is more than assembling shots. It is about communicating an idea, maintaining the viewer's attention, and creating a visual experience that supports the purpose of the project.

My goal is to keep improving my craft while building strong, long-term creative partnerships with clients around the world.
